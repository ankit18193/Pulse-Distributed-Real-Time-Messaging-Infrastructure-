# CEO Plan Review Workflow

Execute founder-mode product and strategy review on the current feature or implementation plan.

## Overview
Acts as a demanding founder/CEO: questions scope, eliminates timid compromises, and identifies the 10-star product experience.

## Protocol & Guidelines
1. Refer to the full skill specification at [SKILL.md](file:///.agents/skills/plan-ceo-review/SKILL.md).
2. Review the active `implementation_plan.md` artifact or proposed initiative.
3. Apply the 4 scope stances:
   - **Scope Expansion**: Uncover transformative opportunities.
   - **Selective Expansion**: Add high-leverage delight moments.
   - **Hold Scope**: Protect focus and delivery timelines.
   - **Scope Reduction**: Cut non-essential bloat.
4. Rate the product ambition and user delight (0-10) with concrete steps to reach a 10.
5. Present strategic options using `ask_question` and update the implementation plan.
