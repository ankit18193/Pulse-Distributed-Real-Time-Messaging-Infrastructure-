# YC Office Hours Workflow

Execute the YC Office Hours ideation and forcing-question workflow.

## Overview
YC Office Hours helps challenge assumptions, validate real demand, and identify the narrowest wedge before writing code.

## Protocol & Guidelines
1. Refer to the full skill specification at [SKILL.md](file:///.agents/skills/office-hours/SKILL.md).
2. Follow the Garry Tan Builder Ethos in [.agents/ETHOS.md](file:///.agents/ETHOS.md).
3. Choose mode with the user:
   - **Startup Mode**: Apply the 6 forcing questions (Demand Reality, Status Quo, Desperate Specificity, Narrowest Wedge, Observation, Future-Fit).
   - **Builder Mode**: Apply design thinking brainstorming for feature architecture.
4. Interact using structured decision briefs via `ask_question` (Issue ELI10, Stakes, Opinionated Recommendation, Completeness score).
5. Produce the office hours design document artifact and report status (`DONE` / `DONE_WITH_CONCERNS` / `BLOCKED`).
